# Overlay Generation

Based on the cleaned transcript, design a CapCut overlay plan.

Editing style:
- modern YouTube tutorial
- premium UI / liquid-glass visual language
- dark neutral palette with selective accent colors
- left/right side information cards
- subtle lower banners
- animated keyword callouts
- no meme-style clutter

For each overlay beat, output:
- timestamp in and out
- purpose of overlay
- exact on-screen text
- visual form: left card | right card | bottom banner | floating label |
  split composition (pseudo-split: speaker reframed + glass panel) | end card
- CapCut-friendly animation suggestion
- color direction
- notes to avoid covering the speaker

Do not suggest overlays for every line. Only choose the strongest moments.
