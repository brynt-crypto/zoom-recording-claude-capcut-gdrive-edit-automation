# Subtitle Treatment

Create a subtitle treatment plan for CapCut Desktop. Goal: make subtitles feel
like a polished YouTube tutorial.

Rules:
- 1 to 2 lines max
- keep phrases short and readable
- break lines on natural phrase boundaries
- avoid huge subtitle blocks
- maintain lower-third safe margin
- do not cover the speaker's mouth or important overlays
- highlight only key words, not every word (NOTE: v1 emphasis is LINE-LEVEL —
  a whole key line gets the accent treatment; per-word coloring is deferred)
- captions should feel clean and premium, not loud

Output:
1. subtitle style direction
2. font style recommendation (will resolve to an available CapCut font)
3. emphasis rules
4. when to switch from normal subtitles to emphasized subtitles
5. moments where subtitles should move or shrink to accommodate overlays
   (NOTE: when a bottom banner is active, subtitles auto-shift up)
